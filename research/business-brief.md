## Observations for the system

- No website, no online ordering; everything runs through FB Messenger and phone calls, so orders, dish choices, delivery fees and payment proofs are handled by hand in chats.
- Menu is already productised (fixed packages with "choose X of Y" rules), so it maps cleanly to an online configurator.
- Orders are mostly **pre-orders for events** (date + time + pax matter), not instant meals.
- Delivery fee is zone-based with vehicle size by load, which can be calculated as a quote.
- Trust signals exist: BIR-registered, OR issued, since 2021, 18K followers, 529+ reactions on the menu post.
- Gaps: real food photography (current visuals are AI), customer reviews/testimonials, opening hours, lead time for orders, Lechon Inyuha price, email.
