# Ordering site — surface brief

Scope: the whole public ordering site (single-page app: menu, package builder, order sheet, checkout, confirmation). Visitor mode: **Persuade** (visitor decides and places an order), with Operate-grade clarity inside the order builder and checkout.

Audience/job: event host in Western Leyte, on a phone from Facebook, feeding 12–40+ people on a set date. Action: build an order, get a delivery estimate, pay by bank QR, upload proof, send. Proof on hand: since 2021, BIR-registered with OR, 18K followers, named owner. No testimonials exist, so none are shown. Constraints: logo colours are binding; food photos are AI placeholders until the owner sends real ones; GCash is not accepted; delivery fees are estimates.

Unresolved: owner email for notifications, downpayment %, lead time, real QR images, real photos, Lechon Inyuha price.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the handaan, the fiesta table seen from above. Food sits on banana leaf and your order visibly fills the table. It refuses the delivery-app arrangement of a photo banner, card grid and cart drawer.

OWN-WORLD: Forest-green banana-leaf ground (#1F7535 family) with sunshine-yellow (#FAE02C) prices and actions, lime (#BBE347) highlights, pale-lime (#FBFCC6) tray tags and deep green-black (#0D150D) type. Containers are foil-tray rectangles and round woven bilao. Labels are tray tags. Heavy friendly grotesk display and a warm humanist body face.

STORY: The visitor sees food sized to their headcount, understands packages are priced per pax, picks a package or trays, and watches their table fill. They see the total plus a delivery estimate, pay by QR, upload proof and send. They get an order number and a Messenger handoff.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile first (390): badge and name top-left, Messenger top-right. The headline is a headcount question with pax chips directly under it that filter packages. Below is an overhead strip of real trays on leaf. A sticky yellow table bar at the thumb shows tray count, total and "Order na". Desktop: headline and pax picker left, the overhead table right, filling as items are added.

SIGNATURE: "The table fills." Adding food springs a tray from the tapped item onto the table bar. The opened table sheet shows your trays laid out on leaf.

FORM: Handaan Table, #1 of the safer-register lineup (user-picked, logo colours pinned), seed key f8baf361 (reroll 1, safer).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
